# Weekly Review — Friday Protocol

> Run this every Friday. Takes 15 minutes. Non-negotiable.

---

## Step 1: Update Your CRM (5 min)

Open your job tracker and:
- [ ] Move all applications to correct stage
- [ ] Add any new contacts made this week
- [ ] Flag everything that needs a follow-up (day 5 or day 10)
- [ ] Archive anything dead (no response in 20+ days, closed role)

---

## Step 2: Send Follow-Ups (3 min)

For every application at day 5 with no response → send the Day 5 follow-up template  
For every application at day 10 with no response → send the Day 10 follow-up template  
For any informational interview contact → send a check-in if it's been 2+ weeks

---

## Step 4: Diagnose & Adjust (3 min)

**If application → response rate is below 5%:**
- Problem: CV targeting, ATS optimization, or applying to wrong roles
- Fix: Re-run CV through Jobscan, narrow your IEP, check application quality

**If screen → interview rate is below 30%:**
- Problem: Phone screen performance or mismatch between CV and actual experience
- Fix: Practice phone screen answers, review what questions you're being asked

**If interview → next round rate is below 40%:**
- Problem: Interview performance (STAR stories, answers, engagement)
- Fix: Run 3 more mock interviews, identify the question types you're failing

**If you're getting to final rounds but no offers:**
- Problem: Culture fit signal, compensation mismatch, or one other candidate
- Fix: Ask for feedback after rejections, recalibrate your closing technique

# Job Alert Setup Guide

> Set up once. Never manually browse job boards again.

---

## LinkedIn Job Alerts

1. Go to [linkedin.com/jobs](https://linkedin.com/jobs)
2. Search for your target role + location
3. Click "Set alert" (bell icon)
4. Set frequency: **Daily**
5. Repeat for:
   - Your exact job title
   - 2–3 variant titles (e.g. "ML Engineer", "Machine Learning Engineer", "AI Engineer")
   - Remote variations ("Remote + [country]")

**Recommended saved searches (do 5–8 total):**
- `[Role] [City]`
- `[Role] Remote`
- `[Variant title] [City]`
- `[Role] [Industry keyword]` (e.g. "Product Manager fintech")

---

## Indeed Job Alerts

1. Go to [indeed.com](https://indeed.com)
2. Search your role + location
3. Click "Get job alerts for this search"
4. Enter email, set to **Daily**
5. Repeat for variant titles

---

## Wellfound / AngelList (Startups)

1. Go to [wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com)
2. Create an account
3. Set up your profile with your target role and stage preferences (seed / Series A / etc.)
4. Enable email notifications for new matching roles

---

## Google Alerts — Target Company Monitoring

For each of your top 20 Tier 1 companies:

1. Go to [alerts.google.com](https://alerts.google.com)
2. Create an alert for: `"[Company name]" hiring`
3. Create an alert for: `"[Company name]" careers`
4. Set frequency: **As it happens** (for top 5 companies) or **Weekly digest** (for others)

**Additional useful alerts:**
- `"[Company]" "series [B/C]"` — catch funding rounds (often precede hiring sprees)
- `"[Company]" "[your target role]"` — catch when they post your specific role type
- `"[Founder name]"` — catch when founders are interviewed (often mention team needs)

---

## Greenhouse & Lever Direct Company Monitoring

Many companies use Greenhouse or Lever as their ATS and don't always push to LinkedIn on time.

**Method 1: Direct career page bookmark**
Bookmark the careers pages of your top 20 Tier 1 companies and check them weekly.

**Method 2: Google dork for roles**
Search: `site:greenhouse.io "[Company name]" "[role keyword]"`
Search: `site:lever.co "[Company name]" "[role keyword]"`

**Method 3: RSS feeds**
Some companies' career pages have RSS feeds you can subscribe to.

---

## Daily Alert Processing (10 min max)

1. Open email at morning routine time
2. Scan subject lines — open only relevant alerts
3. For each relevant role:
   - Does it match your IEP? → Add to Teal CRM
   - Does it not match? → Archive, don't read it
4. Close email
5. Do NOT open LinkedIn/Indeed to browse — you've already got what you need
