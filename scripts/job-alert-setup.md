# Job Alert Setup Guide

> Set up once. Never manually browse job boards again.

---

## LinkedIn Job Alerts

1. Go to [linkedin.com/jobs](https://linkedin.com/jobs)
2. Search for your target role + location
3. Click "Set alert" (bell icon)
4. Set frequency: **Daily**
5. Repeat for:
   - Your exact job title
   - 2–3 variant titles (e.g. "ML Engineer", "Machine Learning Engineer", "AI Engineer")
   - Remote variations ("Remote + [country]")

**Recommended saved searches (do 5–8 total):**
- `[Role] [City]`
- `[Role] Remote`
- `[Variant title] [City]`
- `[Role] [Industry keyword]` (e.g. "Product Manager fintech")

---

## Indeed Job Alerts

1. Go to [indeed.com](https://indeed.com)
2. Search your role + location
3. Click "Get job alerts for this search"
4. Enter email, set to **Daily**
5. Repeat for variant titles

---

## Wellfound / AngelList (Startups)

1. Go to [wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com)
2. Create an account and complete your profile
3. Set your target role, stage (seed / Series A / B), and salary preferences
4. Enable email notifications for new matching roles
5. Best for: startup roles, often posted here before LinkedIn

---

## Google Alerts — Target Company Monitoring

For each of your top 20 Tier 1 companies:

1. Go to [alerts.google.com](https://alerts.google.com)
2. Create an alert: `"[Company name]" hiring`
3. Create an alert: `"[Company name]" careers`
4. Frequency: "As it happens" for top 5 / "Weekly digest" for the rest

**Additional high-value alerts:**
```
"[Company]" "series B"          → Catch funding rounds (hiring sprees follow)
"[Company]" "[your role type]"  → Catch when they post your specific role
"[Founder name]" interview      → Founders often mention team needs in press
"[Company]" "we're hiring"      → Direct signals
```

---

## Greenhouse & Lever — Direct Monitoring

Many companies use these ATS platforms and don't always push roles to LinkedIn immediately.

**Method 1: Google dork**
```
site:greenhouse.io "[Company name]" "[role keyword]"
site:lever.co "[Company name]" "[role keyword]"
```

**Method 2: Direct career page bookmarks**
Bookmark the careers page of every Tier 1 company. Review weekly on Mondays.

**Method 3: RSS (advanced)**
Some Greenhouse/Lever pages expose RSS feeds. Use an RSS reader to aggregate.

---

## Daily Alert Processing — 10 Minutes Max

```
Morning routine:
1. Open email
2. Scan alert subject lines
3. Open relevant alerts only
4. For each role:
   - Matches IEP? → Save to Teal CRM
   - Doesn't match? → Archive immediately
5. Close email
6. Do NOT open LinkedIn or Indeed to browse manually
```

This is the discipline that keeps job searching from consuming your day.
