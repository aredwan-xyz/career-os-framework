# Module 08 — The Weekly Cadence

> **Timeline:** Every week, from Day 1  
> **Goal:** The execution system that makes the entire OS run automatically

---

## The Daily 30-Minute Routine

Run this every working day:

| Time | Duration | Activity |
|------|---------|---------|
| Morning | 10 min | CRM review → action all emails → set follow-up reminders |
| Midday | 10 min | LinkedIn engagement → comment on 5 target company/industry posts |
| Evening | 10 min | Review job alert digest → flag 2–3 new roles → queue tomorrow's outreach |

---

## Weekly Targets

| Activity | Weekly Target | Conversion Benchmark |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| Targeted applications | 8–12 | >10% → screening call |
| Hiring manager DMs | 5–8 | >20% → response |
| Referral requests | 2–3 | >50% → response |
| Informational interviews | 1–2 | 100% → attend |
| LinkedIn posts | 3 | >5% → comment rate |
| LinkedIn comments | 20–30 | Builds passive visibility |
| Follow-ups | All pending at day 5+10 | >30% revival rate |
| STAR story practice | 2 stories | Fluency by interview |

**Weekly Friday review (15 min):**
1. Update all CRM stages
2. Send all day 5 and day 10 follow-ups
3. Calculate this week's conversion rates
4. Identify what's working (double down) and what isn't (adjust)
5. Plan next week's target list

## When to Accelerate

**Signs your search is working:**
- 10%+ application → response rate
- 1–2 inbound recruiter messages per week
- Informational interview conversations converting to intros

**Signs to adjust:**
- Response rate below 5% after 30+ applications → Problem is CV tailoring or role targeting
- No responses to DMs → Problem is messaging or finding wrong contacts
- Getting screened out consistently → Problem is interview preparation
- Getting to final rounds but no offers → Problem is interview performance or fit assessment

**The compounding effect:**  
Every module you complete makes the next search faster, warmer, and better-compensated. Your LinkedIn grows. Your STAR stories sharpen. Your network deepens. Your negotiation skills improve. This is a career operating system — not a one-time job search.
