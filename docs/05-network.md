# Module 05 — Network Activation

> **Timeline:** Permanent system, activate in Week 3+  
> **Goal:** Turn your existing relationships into your most powerful hiring engine

---

## Why Networking Dominates

- 70–80% of jobs are filled through networks, never posted publicly
- Referral candidates are hired at 9x the rate of cold applicants
- Weak ties (people you know somewhat) produce more job leads than close contacts — this is proven by [Granovetter's 1973 research](https://sociology.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj9501/f/publications/the_strength_of_weak_ties_and_exch_w_gans.pdf)

---

## The Three Network Tiers

### Tier 1 — Inner Circle (10–20 people)

Former direct managers, close colleagues, mentors.

**Action:**
- Reach out personally (call or personal message, not a form email)
- Be specific: "I'm targeting [role type] at [company stage]. Do you know anyone at [3 company names]?"
- Ask explicitly for: a referral, an intro, or a recommendation
- Offer reciprocity: "Happy to do the same for you whenever it's useful"

**Timing:** Contact in Week 1. These are your fastest-moving leads.

---

### Tier 2 — Extended Network (50–100 people)

Former colleagues from other teams, classmates, conference contacts.

**Action:**
- Send a personalized "catching up" note — not a bulk update
- Mention you're exploring new opportunities naturally, not as the main point
- Ask for intros only when you have a specific target in mind
- Don't spam with a blast announcement — it reads as desperate and gets ignored

---

### Tier 3 — Weak Ties (Everyone Else)

LinkedIn 2nd connections, industry acquaintances, people you've interacted with online.

**Action:**
- Engage with their content first (comment thoughtfully, 2 weeks)
- Then reach out with a specific, low-friction ask: "I noticed you work at X — I'm exploring roles in that space. Would you have 15 minutes for a quick call?"
- These conversations often unlock opportunities you'd never find on job boards

---

## Informational Interviews — 2 Per Week

Book 20-minute calls with people in your target role or company. This is not a covert job interview. It's genuine intelligence gathering. People who help you understand a company or role will remember you when a role opens.

### How to Request One

**Template:**
```
Hi [Name] — I've been following your work at [Company] and I'm particularly interested 
in how your team approaches [specific thing].

I'm a [role] exploring [what you're looking for] and would love 15–20 minutes of your 
perspective. No agenda — just genuine curiosity.

Would any slot in the next 2 weeks work? I'm happy to work around your schedule.
```

> **Full AI prompt:** See [`prompts/outreach.md`](../prompts/outreach.md)

### 5 Questions That Extract Maximum Value

1. "What does success look like in this role in the first 6 months?"
2. "What's the biggest challenge your team is facing right now?"
3. "How do people typically get hired here — what makes the difference between someone who gets an offer and someone who doesn't?"
4. "Who else should I talk to as I explore this space?"
5. "What would you do differently if you were starting in this field today?"

### After the Call
- Send a thank-you note within 24 hours
- Mention one specific thing from the conversation
- Stay in touch quarterly — send them an article relevant to something you discussed

## Community & Event Leverage

### Online Communities
Join 3–5 niche Slack or Discord communities in your field. They're often where jobs are posted before they reach LinkedIn.

**Best places to find them:**
- Slack communities: search "[your field] Slack community"
- Reddit: relevant subreddits for your role/industry
- Discord: communities around specific tools or technologies you use
- Indie Hackers, Dev.to, Product Coalition, UX Collective (varies by field)

**Participation rules:**
- Answer questions, share wins, give before asking
- Don't lurk — people hire from communities they're active in
- When a job is posted, you want your name to already be familiar

### In-Person Events
Attend at minimum one meetup or conference per month.

**Pro strategy:** Speak, even briefly. A 5-minute lightning talk puts you in a categorically different position than being an attendee. Reach out to organizers 4–6 weeks before events to request a speaking slot.

**Post-event follow-up:**
Send a LinkedIn connection request with a message referencing something you discussed — within 24 hours, while you're still fresh in their memory.
