# AI Prompts — Cover Letters

---

## 1. Core Cover Letter Generator

```
Write a cover letter for this application.

Company: [company name]
Role: [job title]
Key challenge they're facing (from JD or research): [1–2 sentences]
My most relevant achievement: 
  - Context: [1 sentence]
  - What I did: [1 sentence]
  - Result: [metric + timeframe]
Why I'm interested in THIS company specifically: [genuine reason, specific detail]

Requirements:
- Do NOT start with "I am excited to apply for..."
- Paragraph 1: One sentence that proves I've researched them (use the company name + specific detail)
- Paragraph 2: One STAR story (80–100 words max), with a metric
- Paragraph 3: Confident close — one sentence on fit, one requesting the conversation
- Total length: under 250 words
- Tone: confident, direct, zero fluff
- Do not use: "passionate", "hardworking", "team player", "self-starter"
```

---

## 2. Cover Letter Paragraph 1 — Hook Generator

```
Write 5 opening sentences for a cover letter to [company name] applying for [role].

Specific things I know about this company:
- [fact 1 from research]
- [fact 2]
- [fact 3]

Requirements:
- Each sentence must prove I've researched the company
- Not "I'm excited to apply" or "I've always admired your company"
- Reference something specific: a product, a challenge, recent news, their mission
- Under 30 words each
- 5 different angles (product, growth, team, mission, market)
```

---

## 3. Cold Email / Cover Letter Hybrid

```
Write a short cold email (not a formal cover letter) for this situation:

Company: [name]
Role: [title or "they're not actively hiring but I want in"]
My connection or research point: [something specific about them]
My best relevant result: [metric]

Requirements:
- Subject line: compelling, specific, under 8 words
- Body: 4 sentences max
- Sentence 1: Why I'm reaching out to THEM specifically
- Sentence 2: One specific credential or result
- Sentence 3: The ask (call, quick chat)
- Sentence 4: Easy out ("no pressure if timing's off")
- Tone: human, peer-to-peer — not "dear hiring team"
```
