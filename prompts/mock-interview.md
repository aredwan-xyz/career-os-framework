# AI Prompts — Mock Interviews & Thank You Emails

---

## Mock Interview — Full Simulation

```
You are a tough, experienced interviewer hiring for the following role:

Company: [company name]
Role: [job title]
Level: [junior / mid / senior / lead / director]
Key things this role needs: [3 key requirements from the JD]

Interview me. Ask me exactly 6 questions in this order:
1. A behavioral question (STAR format expected)
2. A situational question (hypothetical scenario)
3. Another behavioral question focused on a challenge or failure
4. A technical or domain-knowledge question specific to this role
5. A strategic or big-picture question ("where do you see this field in 5 years", etc.)
6. A curveball — something unexpected that tests thinking under pressure

After I answer each question:
- Score my answer 1–10
- Tell me what was strong (be specific)
- Tell me what was weak or missing (be specific)
- Suggest one improvement I can make right now

Be harsh. I'd rather struggle here than in the real interview.

[After I answer all 6, give me an overall assessment and top 3 areas to improve]
```

## Mock Interview — Behavioral Questions Only

```
Give me 10 behavioral interview questions for a [role] at a [company type].

For each question:
- Write the question
- Tell me what the interviewer is really trying to assess
- Give me the 1 thing that separates a great answer from an average one
- Give me an example of a strong answer structure (don't write the full answer — I'll do that)

Make the questions progressively harder. Start with common ones and end with questions 
that most candidates find difficult.
```

## Mock Interview — Technical / Domain Round

```
You are a senior [role] interviewing a candidate for a [role] position.

Ask me 5 technical or domain-knowledge questions appropriate for a [level] candidate.

After each answer:
- Tell me if I got it right, partially right, or wrong
- Fill in anything I missed
- Rate the answer as: entry-level / mid-level / senior-level quality

Topics to cover: [list 3–5 relevant technical areas for your role]
```

## STAR Story Sharpener

```
I'm going to tell you a STAR story from my experience. After I tell it, please:
1. Score it 1–10 on: Clarity / Relevance / Impact / Conciseness
2. Identify: what's missing, what's vague, what should be cut
3. Rewrite the Result section to be stronger
4. Suggest 3 different questions this story could be used to answer
5. Tell me the one thing that would make this story 2x more compelling

My story:
[paste your story]
```

## Thank You Email Generator

```
Write a post-interview thank you email.

Interviewer name and role: [Name, Title]
Company: [company]
Role I interviewed for: [role]
One specific thing from our conversation: [quote, topic, or exchange you remember]
One new thought or value-add I want to add: [an insight, a relevant article, or an idea]

Requirements:
- Subject: "Thank you — [Role] interview" (or a creative variant)
- Open by referencing the specific conversation moment
- 2–3 sentences max on why I'm interested after the conversation
- Include the new value-add naturally (not forced)
- Close confidently — not "I hope to hear from you" — something more assertive
- Under 120 words total
- Not sycophantic — professional warmth, not gushing
```

## Post-Interview Reflection Prompt

```
Help me do a structured post-interview reflection.

Role: [role]
Company: [company]
Interview format: [phone screen / technical / behavioral / panel / etc.]
How long: [duration]

Questions I was asked:
[list them]

My answers that felt strong:
[describe]

My answers that felt weak or incomplete:
[describe]

Questions I wasn't prepared for:
[list]

Please:
1. Assess where I likely performed well vs. poorly
2. Identify what the weak answers were missing
3. Write improved versions of my 2 weakest answers
4. Predict the 3 most likely objections they'll have about me as a candidate
5. Suggest what I should address proactively in follow-up communication
```
