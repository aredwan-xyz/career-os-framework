# AI Prompts — CV Tailoring & Materials

Copy these prompts directly into Claude or ChatGPT. Replace everything in `[brackets]`.

---

## 1. Skill Gap Analysis

```
I'm targeting roles as: [role title, e.g. "Senior Product Manager at Series B SaaS startups"]

My current skills (list everything, rate 1-10):
[paste your skills list]

Here are 5 job descriptions I'm targeting:
[paste JD 1]
---
[paste JD 2]
---
[paste JD 3]
---

Tasks:
1. List the 10 most frequently required skills across all JDs
2. Cross-reference against my skills and identify my gaps
3. Rank gaps by: (a) frequency across JDs and (b) importance to getting hired
4. For each gap, suggest the fastest credible way to address it (course, project, certification)
5. Identify my top 3 "unfair advantage" skills that appear in the JDs and I'm strong at
```

## 2. Master CV Bullet Writer

```
Write 5 strong CV bullet points for this role and company:

Company type: [e.g. Series B fintech startup]
My role: [e.g. Senior ML Engineer]
What I actually did: [describe in plain English what you worked on]
Results: [any numbers, even rough ones]
Timeframe: [e.g. 6 months]

Requirements for each bullet:
- Start with a strong action verb (not "Responsible for" or "Helped")
- Include a specific measurable result (%, $, time saved, users impacted)
- Use language a recruiter at a tech company would recognize
- Maximum 1 line (under 120 characters)
- No buzzwords or vague claims

Write 5 variants — I'll choose the best 2.
```

## 3. CV Tailoring Engine (Core Prompt)

```
I'm applying for this role:
[paste full job description]

Here are my current CV bullets (my master CV):
[paste 20–30 bullets from your master CV]

Please:
1. Select the 12 bullets that best match the JD keywords and priorities
2. Rewrite each selected bullet to mirror the JD's exact language and terminology
   (keep the facts accurate — only change the framing and words)
3. Write a 3-sentence executive summary for the top of my CV:
   - Sentence 1: My role and niche
   - Sentence 2: My best measurable outcome, relevant to this JD
   - Sentence 3: What I'm bringing to THIS company specifically (mention them by name)
4. List the top 7 keywords I must include for this ATS to pass
5. Flag any hard requirements in the JD I don't meet, and suggest how to address each
```

## 4. ATS Keyword Injection

```
Here is my CV:
[paste CV text]

Here is the job description:
[paste JD]

Tasks:
1. List every keyword in the JD I'm missing from my CV
2. Suggest natural places in my CV where each keyword can be added authentically
3. Rewrite any bullets I need to update with the keywords embedded
4. Score my current CV match as a percentage and explain what's dragging it down
```

## 5. Executive Summary Generator

```
Write a 3-sentence executive summary for the top of my CV.

My background: [2–3 sentences about your experience]
Target role: [specific role at specific company type]
Best result I've achieved: [your most impressive metric]
What this company is building/solving (from research): [1 sentence]

Requirements:
- Sentence 1: My role + niche + years of experience
- Sentence 2: Specific outcome or achievement relevant to this role
- Sentence 3: What I'm bringing to this company (mention the company type)
- No clichés: no "results-driven", "passionate", "self-starter", "team player"
- Should make a hiring manager want to keep reading
```

## 6. LinkedIn Headline Generator

```
Write 5 LinkedIn headline options for me.

My role: [current or target role]
My niche: [what problems I solve / who I solve them for]
My biggest credential: [best result, certification, or company name]
Keywords I need to appear for: [3–5 keywords]

Requirements:
- Under 200 characters
- No generic phrases like "passionate professional" or "seeking opportunities"
- Include at least one keyword from my list
- Make the value proposition clear at a glance
- Write 5 different variants (different emphasis each time)
```

## 7. LinkedIn About Section Writer

```
Write my LinkedIn About section.

My background: [2–3 sentences]
My niche: [specific positioning statement]
Who I help: [type of company]
How I help them: [method/approach]
Best results I've delivered: [2–3 metrics]
What I'm looking for now: [type of role/company]
CTA: [how people should reach out — DM, email, etc.]

Requirements:
- Open with a hook — a result or bold observation, NOT "I am a..."
- 3–4 short paragraphs (not one wall of text)
- First-person, direct voice — no corporate speak
- End with a clear call to action
- Under 400 words
```
