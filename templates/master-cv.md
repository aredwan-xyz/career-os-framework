## CV Tailoring Instructions

When creating a tailored CV from this master:

1. Read the JD carefully — identify the 3 most important outcome areas
2. Filter your master bullets by relevant tags
3. Select 12–15 bullets that best match
4. Paste JD + selected bullets into the CV Tailoring AI prompt
5. Swap in AI-suggested rewrites
6. Select the matching executive summary variant (or generate a new one)
7. ATS-check on Jobscan (target: 85+)
8. Save as `FirstName-LastName-CV-CompanyName.pdf`
