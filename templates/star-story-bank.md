# STAR Story Bank

> Build 15 stories. Practice each until you can tell it in 90 seconds cold.
> Update this file monthly — you forget wins faster than you think.

---

## How to Use This Bank

**In behavioral interviews:** Match the question type to a story below. Each story is tagged with the question types it covers.

**Practice method:** Read the story once. Then close the doc and tell it out loud. Time yourself. Target: 75–90 seconds. Shorter is better than longer.

**Variation tip:** The same story can often be adapted for multiple questions — just change which element you emphasize. A project success story can also be a leadership story, a teamwork story, or a failure story (by focusing on what went wrong and what you learned).

```
STORY #[n] — [Short title, e.g. "Rebuilt the fraud detection pipeline"]

TAGS: [R] Revenue  [T] Technical  [L] Leadership  [P] Process  [C] Customer  [D] Data
QUESTION TYPES THIS STORY COVERS: [list 3–5 common behavioral questions]
TELL TIME: [target 75–90 seconds]

──────────────────────────────────────────────

SITUATION (1–2 sentences)
What was the context? What was at stake?
→ 

TASK (1 sentence)
What were you specifically responsible for?
→ 

ACTION (3–5 sentences — most detailed section)
What did you do? What decisions did you make and why?
→ 

RESULT (2–3 sentences — must include a metric)
What happened? How was it measured?
→ 

LESSON / WHAT I'D DO DIFFERENTLY (1 sentence)
→ 

──────────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Category Targets

Build at least 5 stories in each category:

### Category A: Impact / Results Stories (5 stories)
Stories where you moved a metric, shipped a product, or created measurable value.

Required elements for each:
- A before-and-after comparison
- A specific percentage, dollar amount, time saved, or user count
- Your individual contribution (not the team's)

---

### Category B: Challenge / Failure Stories (5 stories)
Stories where things went wrong, you made a mistake, or faced a major obstacle.

Required elements for each:
- What specifically went wrong (be honest)
- How you responded in the moment
- What you'd do differently now
- A genuine lesson (not a "weakness that's actually a strength")

---

### Category C: Leadership & Influence Stories (5 stories)
Stories where you led, mentored, influenced, or changed direction without formal authority.

Required elements for each:
- The stakeholder landscape (who disagreed, who you needed to move)
- Your specific approach to influence
- The outcome (did it work? what was the result?)
- What you learned about leadership

---

## Pre-Interview Story Prep Checklist

Before every interview:

- [ ] Read through all 15 stories once
- [ ] Identify the 5 stories most relevant to this specific role
- [ ] Practice those 5 out loud (not just in your head)
- [ ] Time yourself — keep each under 90 seconds
- [ ] Prepare 2 "failure" stories (most interviewers ask at least one)
- [ ] Have a "greatest achievement" story ready — they almost always ask
- [ ] Know your numbers cold — don't fumble the metrics mid-story

## AI Prompt — Story Sharpener

```
I'm going to tell you a STAR story. After I tell it, please:
1. Score it 1–10 on: Clarity / Relevance / Impact / Conciseness
2. Identify what's missing or vague
3. Suggest what I should cut (most stories are 30% too long)
4. Rewrite the RESULT section to be more impactful
5. Suggest 3 different interview questions this story could answer
6. Tell me the ONE thing that would make this story significantly more compelling

Here's my story:
[paste your story]
```
