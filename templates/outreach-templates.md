# Outreach Templates

> Personalize every message. These are frameworks, not copy-paste scripts.
> The [brackets] are mandatory replacements — never send with brackets intact.

---

## LinkedIn DM Templates

### 1. Post-Application to Hiring Manager

```
Hi [Name] — I just applied for the [Role] on your team.

I've spent the last [X years] helping [company type like theirs] [specific outcome 
relevant to the role], most recently [specific result with a number] at [company].

Wanted to put a face to the application. Happy to share more if useful — 
either way, best of luck with the search.

[Your First Name]
```

**When to send:** 24–48 hours after applying  
**Word count target:** Under 75 words  
**Do not:** Ask for a job, say you're "excited," or list all your achievements

---

### 2. Cold Outreach — No Open Role

```
Hi [Name] — I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific initiative/product] 
closely, particularly [specific detail that shows you've actually done research].

I'm a [role] who [specific result in their relevant area]. The kind of [problem] 
your team is probably thinking about as you scale.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call? No agenda, just genuine curiosity 
about how you're approaching [relevant challenge].

[Your First Name]
```

**When to send:** When targeting a company before a role opens  
**Personalization required:** The specific detail in line 1 — this is what gets replies

---

### 3. Referral Request — Warm Connection

```
Hey [Name] — hope things are going well at [Company].

I'm exploring a move into [space] and noticed you're there. I'm targeting 
[type of role] and [Company] is high on my list.

Would you be open to: (a) an informal intro to someone on the [team], or 
(b) a referral if there's a role that fits?

Happy to return the favor anytime — and totally fine if the timing's off.

[Your Name]
```

---

### 4. Referral Request — Weak Connection

```
Hey [Name] — we connected [context — e.g. "at the ProductCon meetup last year"] 
and I've been following your work at [Company] since.

I'm exploring [role type] opportunities and [Company] is at the top of my list 
because [specific genuine reason]. I know it's a big ask, but if you'd be open 
to a referral or intro to the hiring team, I'd genuinely appreciate it.

Completely fine either way — no pressure.

[Your First Name]
```

---

### 5. Follow-Up — Day 5, No Response

```
Hi [Name] — just following up on my application for [Role].

Still very interested and happy to answer any questions about my background.

[Your Name]
```

**Rule:** Short and confident. Do not re-pitch. Do not apologize for following up.

---

### 6. Follow-Up — Day 10, Still No Response

```
Hi [Name] — one last nudge on [Role].

I came across [brief relevant observation about their business — a product launch, 
a news item, a challenge they're facing] and it made me even more interested 
in contributing to it.

Happy to connect whenever it makes sense.

[Your Name]
```

**After day 10:** Move to "passive" in your CRM. Check quarterly.

---

### 7. Informational Interview Request

```
Hi [Name] — I've been following your work at [Company] closely, 
particularly [specific project/initiative/post they wrote].

I'm a [role] exploring [what you're targeting] and your perspective 
on [specific topic] would be really valuable to me.

Would you have 15–20 minutes for a call? Happy to work around your schedule.

[Your First Name]
```

**Note:** Be genuinely curious — these calls are for intelligence and relationship building, not covert job pitches.

## Email Templates

### Direct Outreach Email (Alternative to LinkedIn)

```
Subject: [Role Type] with background in [your niche] — [Company]

Hi [Name],

[Hook sentence: something specific about the company that you genuinely find interesting]

I'm a [role] who [specific result]. Most recently, [key achievement in 1 sentence].

I'd love a 15-minute call to learn more about [Company]'s approach to [relevant challenge]. 
Happy to work around your schedule.

[Your Name]
[LinkedIn URL]
[Portfolio if relevant]
```

---

### Reachout After Being Rejected

```
Subject: Re: [Role] — Thank you

Hi [Name],

Thank you for letting me know. I have a lot of respect for the process you run, 
and I remain genuinely interested in [Company]'s work.

If it would be appropriate, I'd welcome any feedback on where I fell short — 
it would help me improve significantly.

And if any other roles come up that might be a fit, I'd be glad to hear about them.

[Your Name]
```

**Why this matters:** Most rejected candidates disappear. Gracious rejection responses are remembered — and sometimes lead to roles months later.

## Networking Event Follow-Up

```
Hi [Name] — great to meet you at [Event] [yesterday / last week].

[Reference one specific thing you discussed]

I'd love to stay in touch — would a [LinkedIn connection / brief call] make sense?

[Your Name]
```

**Send within 24 hours while you're fresh in their memory.**
