# Cover Letter Template

> One page maximum. Personalize paragraphs 1 and 2 for every application.
> Paragraph 3 can be kept mostly constant with minor adjustments.

---

## The 3-Paragraph Framework

---

**[Your Name]**  
[City, Country] | [email] | [LinkedIn] | [Portfolio]

[Date]

[Hiring Manager Name, if known]  
[Title]  
[Company Name]

---

Dear [Name / Hiring Manager],

**[PARAGRAPH 1 — The Hook: Why them, specifically]**

[One to two sentences that prove you've done your research. Reference something specific:
- A product feature or recent launch
- A challenge they've publicly discussed
- A recent press mention or funding round
- Something from a blog post, interview, or tweet by the founder/team
- Their specific mission or approach to a problem

NEVER start with: "I am excited to apply for..." or "I have always admired your company..."
ALWAYS reference the company name and something specific about them.]

**[PARAGRAPH 2 — The Proof: Your best STAR story]**

[One concrete example directly relevant to their biggest challenge. Format: Situation (1 sentence) → Action (1–2 sentences) → Result (1 sentence with a number). Total: 80–100 words maximum.]

**[PARAGRAPH 3 — The Close: Confident and specific]**

[One sentence on why your background maps directly to their situation. One sentence requesting a conversation — specific and confident, not hopeful. Not "I hope to hear from you." Something like: "I'd welcome 20 minutes to explore this further — happy to work around your schedule."]

Sincerely,

[Your Name]

## Example — Filled In

---

**Jordan Lee**  
San Francisco, CA | jordan@email.com | linkedin.com/in/jordanlee

March 15, 2025

Sarah Chen  
Head of Engineering  
Meridian AI

---

Dear Sarah,

Meridian's recent architectural shift from batch to real-time inference — detailed in your engineering blog last month — is exactly the infrastructure challenge I've been solving since 2021. The trade-offs your team navigated between latency, cost, and accuracy are ones I've lived through at two previous companies.

At Axiom (Series B, 120 engineers), I led the migration of our fraud detection pipeline from a 6-hour batch cycle to a sub-50ms real-time system. The core challenge was reducing model size without sacrificing recall — we achieved a 40% latency reduction while maintaining 99.2% recall, ultimately saving $32K/month in cloud costs and enabling a product capability our sales team had been requesting for 18 months.

Given Meridian's focus on production ML at scale, this feels like a natural fit. I'd welcome 20 minutes to explore this further — happy to work around your schedule.

Sincerely,

Jordan Lee

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Starting with "I am excited..." | Start with something specific about them |
| Summarizing your CV | Tell one STAR story instead |
| Vague claims ("I am passionate...") | Replace with specific results |
| Addressing "Dear Hiring Manager" | Find the name on LinkedIn |
| Generic close ("I hope to hear from you") | Use a confident, specific request |
| More than one page | Cut ruthlessly — 3 paragraphs is enough |
| No company name in paragraph 1 | Always name the company you're writing to |
| Focusing on what you want | Focus on what you bring to their problem |
